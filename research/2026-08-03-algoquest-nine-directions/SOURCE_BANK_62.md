## Use rules

- Prefer official documentation and standards for implementation claims.
- Treat product pages and historical gamebook sites as design references, not evidence of educational efficacy.
- Treat reviews and studies as questions to test in AlgoQuest, not as proof that a feature works for every learner.
- Cite a source beside each non-trivial factual claim in the daily article.
